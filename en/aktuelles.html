23 September 2026 · Book launch

New publication: Praktiken des Erziehens

The volume Praktiken des Erziehens. Zugänge zum Allgemeinen der Erziehung und Erziehungswissenschaft, edited by Johannes Türstig, Martin Weber-Spanknebel and Malte Brinkmann, has been published by Springer VS. It brings together different theoretical and empirical approaches to educational practices.

My contribution, Zeigen als polykontexturale Praxis des Erziehens [Showing as a polycontextural practice of education], appears on pages 385–416.

The book was celebrated at a launch at the Institute of Education at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin on 23 September 2026. Presentations by Dietrich Benner, Arnd-Michael Nohl, Cornelie Dietrich, Johannes Türstig and Malte Brinkmann were framed and discussed by Gert Biesta.

17 September 2026

Joint keynote at the Luhmann Conference in Dubrovnik

At the Luhmann Conference 2026, I gave a joint keynote with Prof. Dr. Franz Kasper Krönig (TH Köln) on 17 September: Toward a Post-Transcendental Concept of Meaning. Elaborating the Immanent Necessity of Luhmann’s Theory of Meaning. The conference, entitled “Meaning. Observed with social systems theory”, took place at the Inter-University Centre Dubrovnik from 14 to 18 September.

Our contribution asked whether Luhmann’s factual, temporal and social dimensions can be reconstructed as necessary structures of meaning. The starting point is that any attempt to analyse or question meaning itself operates within meaning. We discussed the implications of this approach for a post-transcendental reading of Luhmann’s theory and the open question of whether spatiality might constitute a further dimension.

9 June 2026

Educational theory under observation: presentation at the “Luhmann 100” pre-conference

On 9 June 2026, I presented Under Observation: Anomalies of Educational Theory. On the Idiosyncratic Reception of Luhmannian Systems Theory in General Pedagogy in the “Structure and Semantics” panel at Zeppelin University in Friedrichshafen. The pre-conference Anschlüsse und blinde Flecken. Systemtheoretische Beobachtungen der Gegenwart took place on 8–9 June as part of Luhmann 100: Zur Gegenwart eines Theorieprogramms.

What happens when humanist educational thought encounters the anti-humanist premises of Luhmann’s systems theory? Drawing on my doctoral research, the presentation examined how general pedagogy appropriates and transforms systems-theoretical concepts. It focused on constructions of the educandus: how educational texts imagine those to be educated and attribute social positions, expectations and possibilities to them.

A case analysis explored how claims to scientific truth, practical effectiveness and normative desirability become intertwined. Rather than simply assessing whether Luhmann has been received “correctly”, the analysis asked what problems these reinterpretations address and what forms of pedagogical knowledge they produce. This opened up a perspective on education as communicative work on social persons and addresses, without equating these constructions with individual human beings.

The main conference ran from 9 to 12 June 2026. A conference volume is announced for publication by Suhrkamp in 2027, marking the centenary of Niklas Luhmann’s birth.

Retrospective · 30 April 2026

Lecture and concluding discussion in the EN:DIRA lecture series

On 30 April 2026, I gave the online lecture How Can the Boundary between “Left/Right” Be Defined? — Definitional Competence as a Mark of Professionalism as part of the EN:DIRA lecture series Brandmauern & Randmauern. Analyse, Kritik und Handlungsoptionen in Bildungspraxis, Erziehungswissenschaften und Sozialer Arbeit. EN:DIRA stands for Erziehungswissenschaftliches Netzwerk: Diskursintervention gegen Rechtsaußen.

The lecture explored how the terms “politically left” and “politically right” can be defined and how such distinctions can be justified and critically examined. Using case vignettes, it drew on systems theory (Luhmann/Fuchs) and Critical Theory (Adorno) to explore definitional competence in critical social work and pedagogy.

On 6 July 2026, I also participated as a discussant in the concluding discussion of the lecture series, together with Prof. Wolfgang Meseth (Goethe University Frankfurt am Main) and other speakers.

An archival copy of the lecture recording is available on my YouTube channel (in German).
