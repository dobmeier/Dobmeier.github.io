Research Interests

Semantic-analytical research on society(esp. the pedagogisation of social problems and the de-/limitation of pedagogical forms)
Qualitative research on Erziehung(esp. negative anthropologies of the Educandus between humanism and antihumanism, didactic practices of showing (Zeigen) and normativity)
Philosophy and social studies of science(esp. casuistic theory work, empirical research on theory, and theoretical empirical research)
Social, social-work and pedagogical operativity(esp. the professionalisation of differential capacities for observation, case diagnosis and definition)
Difference-theoretical epistemologies(esp. Luhmann's systems theory, Adorno's critical theory and poststructuralist approaches)
Agnotology in the theory of Bildung(esp. the politics and aesthetics of knowing and not-knowing within pedagogical orders)

Teaching Materials

Academic writing is not a technique to be memorised — it is a form of thinking that has to be worked out. A set of materials accompanying that process, from the first thematic interest to the finished term paper, is available on this site: step-by-step guides, worked examples of introductions, a four-part video course, and a fifty-point self-assessment checklist with annotated examples of common mistakes.

These materials are written for students at German-speaking universities and follow the formal conventions of German academic writing (Hausarbeit), so they are available in German only.
